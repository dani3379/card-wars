5. PUBLIC-DOMAIN FINE ART
=========================

Some card and creature illustrations adapt reproductions of public-domain
works by artists including Gustave Dore, Mikhail Vrubel, Henry Fuseli,
Francisco Goya, Pieter Bruegel, and Hieronymus Bosch. These artists are also
credited in the in-game Credits screen. Public-domain status can vary by
country and by reproduction; release provenance should retain the source
record for every included image.
