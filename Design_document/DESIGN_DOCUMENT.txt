BURNING MEADOW — Game Design Document (v1.0 Implementation)
=============================================================

1. OVERVIEW
-----------

Burning Meadow is a lane-based combat roguelike deckbuilder built in Godot 4.6.
The player navigates a branching map across 3 acts, fighting premade encounters
in a 4-lane simultaneous combat system. Inspired by Card Wars (lane combat,
floop abilities) and Slay the Spire (roguelike meta-progression, map structure,
shop/rest/event nodes).

Core Loop:
  Main Menu -> Pick Starting Relic -> Map -> Combat/Shop/Rest/Event ->
  Card Reward -> Map -> ... -> Boss -> Next Act -> ... -> Final Boss (Act 3) ->
  Victory/Game Over

3. COMBAT SYSTEM
-----------------

3.1 Board Layout

  4 lanes, each with a player slot (bottom) and enemy slot (top). Creatures are
  placed in specific lanes and fight the creature directly opposing them. Empty
  lanes allow face damage.

3.2 Round Flow (Current Implementation)

  ROUND START
    |-- Keyword effects: Regenerate heals 1, Wither reduces ATK
    |-- Encounter passive start-of-round triggers
    |-- Enemy places 1-2 creatures (before player acts)
    |-- Mana refills to base + boss relic bonuses (+ up to 1 banked)
    |-- Player draws 4 cards (hand sorted by cost)
    +-- PLAYER TURN begins

  PLAYER TURN (free actions in any order)
    |-- Play creatures from hand (drag above play line)
    |     +-- Lane selected by mouse position, rejects occupied lanes
    |-- Play spells (drag above play line, then click target)
    |-- Toggle Floop on battlefield creatures (click)
    |     +-- Flooping PREVENTS the creature from attacking this turn
    |-- Sacrifice once per turn (S key or button)
    +-- End Turn (E key or button)

  END TURN
    |-- Resolve Floop abilities (flooped creatures skip combat)
    |-- SWIFT PHASE: All Swift creatures attack first
    |-- SEQUENTIAL PHASE: Player creatures attack (lanes 0->3), then enemy
    |     creatures attack (lanes 0->3). If player kills enemy, it doesn't
    |     retaliate. Dead creatures are removed from the fight entirely.
    |-- Ranged creatures attack random targets
    |-- Piercing excess -> face damage
    |-- Thorns reflect damage
    |-- Deaths processed, on-death effects trigger
    |-- Berserker growth (+1 ATK if attacked)
    |-- Cleanup dead creatures (REMOVED from fight, not recycled)
    |-- Discard hand (Retain cards kept)
    |-- Assassin self-destruct check
    |-- Encounter end-of-round passives
    |-- Check win/loss
    +-- Next round starts

3.3 Combat Phases Detail

  SWIFT PHASE: Creatures with Swift keyword attack before sequential combat.
  Both player and enemy Swift creatures resolve in lane order (0->3).

  SEQUENTIAL PHASE: Player creatures attack first (lanes 0->3), then enemy
  creatures attack (lanes 0->3). If a player creature kills its opposing enemy,
  that enemy does NOT retaliate. If a lane has only one creature and the opposing
  lane was empty at combat start, that creature deals face damage.

  RANGED: Ranged creatures skip normal lane combat. Instead they attack a random
  enemy creature. Their lane counts as empty for face damage purposes.

  PIERCING: When a creature with Piercing kills its opponent, excess damage
  carries through to the enemy hero's HP.

  THORNS: When a creature with Thorns is attacked, it deals 1 damage back to
  the attacker (bypasses Armored).

3.4 Sacrifice System

  Once per turn, the player can sacrifice a friendly creature on the
  battlefield. This triggers the creature's on-death effect. Activated via S key
  or HUD button. Visual: valid sacrifice targets get red border highlight during
  sacrifice mode.

3.5 Floop System

  Creatures with a Floop ability can be toggled on the battlefield by clicking
  them. A flooping creature CANNOT ATTACK that turn — it activates its special
  ability instead. This makes every floop a real decision: use the ability or
  deal combat damage. Visual: blue "FLOOP" indicator and blue border.

  Floop Types:
    damage_any ............. Deal X damage to a random enemy creature
    damage_opposing ........ Deal X damage to opposing creature
    summon_random .......... Summon X/Y token in a random empty lane
    kill_adjacent_summon ... Kill adjacent friendly, summon X/Y in its place
    steal_atk .............. Steal 1 ATK from opposing creature
    heal_all_friendly ...... Heal all friendly creatures by X
    heal_self .............. Heal self by X
    summon_token ........... Summon X/Y token in a random empty lane
    buff_adjacent_atk ...... Adjacent friendly creatures +X ATK this turn
    gain_mana .............. Gain X mana this turn
    grow_atk ............... +X ATK permanently

3.6 Spell System

  Spells are played from hand like creatures (drag above play line). Targeted
  spells enter targeting mode -- click a valid target to resolve. Non-targeted
  spells resolve immediately.

  Targeting Types:
    none ................... Resolves immediately (AoE, self-buff, draw)
    enemy_creature ......... Must click an enemy creature
    friendly_creature ...... Must click a friendly creature
    any_creature ........... Click any creature on the board
    any .................... Click any creature or hero

  Spell Effect Types:
    damage ................. Deal X to target creature
    damage_face ............ Deal X to enemy hero
    damage_all_enemies ..... Deal X to all enemy creatures
    damage_all ............. Deal X to ALL creatures (both sides)
    buff_atk ............... Target creature +X ATK (permanent or this-turn)
    buff_hp ................ Target creature +X HP
    buff_all_atk ........... All friendly creatures +X ATK
    heal ................... Remove X damage from target creature
    draw ................... Draw X cards
    custom ................. Special handler in Combat.gd

  Custom Spells: shove, gambit, blood_tithe, reckless_charge, scrap, barricade,
  second_wind, reposition, lightning, offering, grave_pact, fuel_the_pyre,
  pillage, echo_spell, kings_command, unholy_bargain, mass_grave, dark_pact,
  war_chant, grave_robbery, cataclysm, soul_swap, apocalypse, adrenaline,
  bloodletting, turbo, concentrate, recycle

3.7 Deck System

  Draw pile and discard pile store card IDs (strings). When a card is drawn,
  CardDB.get_card_data(id) is called to get the card data.

    - Dead player creatures are REMOVED from the fight entirely (not recycled)
    - Discarded hand cards go to discard pile (for deck recycling)
    - Exhausted spells go to exhaust pile (removed from fight)
    - Retained cards stay in hand at end of turn
    - When draw pile is empty, discard pile is shuffled into draw pile

  This means your deck shrinks as creatures die in combat. Card draw becomes
  valuable as you race against running out of playable cards.

3.8 Mana System

  Base mana: 3 per turn. Up to 1 unspent mana carries over to the next turn
  (Ice Cream relic removes the cap). Boss relics can permanently increase max
  mana by +1 each, at the cost of a significant downside.

  Mana generation sources:
    - Boss relics: +1 max mana permanently (with downside)
    - Regular relics: Lantern (turn 1 bonus), Happy Flower (every 3 turns),
      Ice Cream (full banking), Art of War (bonus if no cards played),
      Sundial (+2 every 3 shuffles)
    - Spell cards: Adrenaline, Bloodletting, Turbo, Concentrate, Recycle,
      Offering, Scrap, War Chant
    - Creature floops: Mana Sprite (gain 1), Leyline Conduit (gain 2)
    - Creature passive: Leyline Conduit (+1 mana per turn while alive)

3.9 Enemy AI

  Enemies place 1-2 creatures per round from a premade ordered deck (from
  EncounterDB). Placement prefers empty lanes, targeting: opposing occupied
  lanes first, then random empty lanes. When the deck runs out, a reinforcement
  creature is used infinitely.

  Thorns ............ Deals 1 back to anything that attacks it

  Piercing .......... Excess kill damage hits enemy face
  Sacrifice ......... Must kill a friendly creature to play this card
  Exhaust ........... Removed from fight after use (not recycled)
  Retain ............ Kept in hand instead of discarding at end of turn
  Wither ............ Loses ATK at start of each round (min 0)
  On-Enter .......... Effect triggers when creature is placed on board
  On-Death .......... Effect triggers when creature dies
  Floop ............. Skip attack to use special ability (toggle)
  Adj. Buff ......... Neighboring creatures get stat bonus

  On-Enter Types: damage_opposing, damage_opposing_draw, damage_random_player,

5.1 TRIBE SYSTEM (4 Tribes)

  Every drafted creature belongs to one of four tribes: Wild, Undead, Order, or
  Arcane. Starter deck creatures (Footman, Squire, Knight, Conscript) are
  untagged. Tribes have no automatic bonuses -- they are identity tags that
  specific cards reference in their own effects.

  Wild ............... Aggressive, fast, animalistic. High ATK, low HP.
                      Favors face damage and fast kills.
  Undead ............. Death-focused, sacrifice-synergy. On-death effects,
                      summoning from death, snowball scaling.
  Order .............. Defensive, structured, military. High HP, adjacency
                      buffs, damage reduction, board durability.
  Arcane ............. Spell-synergy, abilities, utility. Floop, draw, spell
                      cost reduction, ability scaling.

  Tribal synergy comes from individual cards whose effects reference their
  tribe (e.g. Bannerman: "all Order creatures +1 ATK", Corpse Eater: "+1
  extra ATK if the dead creature was Undead"). No tribe grants passive bonuses
  just for existing in the deck. The player must find tribal payoff cards AND
  draft enough tribal creatures for those payoffs to matter.

  Tribal Payoff Cards:
    Wild ....... Pack Hunter (scales with Wild count), Bloodhound (draws per
                 Wild on field)
    Undead ..... Corpse Eater (extra ATK on Undead death), Gravedigger (draws
                 on Undead death), Pyre Keeper (scales with Undead death)
    Order ...... Bannerman (buffs Order only), Squire Captain (stronger token
                 near Order)
    Arcane ..... Fanatic (ATK = Arcane count), Torchbearer (buffs adj Arcane)

  Tribe Distribution (draft pool):
    Wild ....... 6 common, 5 uncommon, 3 rare  (14 total)
    Undead ..... 2 common, 4 uncommon, 6 rare  (12 total)
    Order ...... 6 common, 5 uncommon, 4 rare  (15 total)
    Arcane ..... 3 common, 5 uncommon, 2 rare  (10 total)

8. MAP & PROGRESSION
----------------------

8.1 Map Structure

  Each act has 8 rows. Player starts at bottom and moves upward one row at a
  time. Each row has 1-3 nodes. Nodes connect to 1-2 nodes in the next row.

  Row Template (per act):
    Row 0: combat, combat
    Row 1: combat, event, combat
    Row 2: rest, shop
    Row 3: combat, combat, event
    Row 4: combat, combat
    Row 5: elite, combat
    Row 6: rest, shop
    Row 7: boss (single node, all paths converge)

8.2 Node Types

  Combat (sword icon) ...... Standard fight from EncounterDB
  Elite (star icon) ........ Harder fight, better rewards
  Boss (skull icon) ........ Act boss, must defeat to advance
  Rest (hot spring icon) ... Heal, upgrade, or remove a card
  Shop (coin icon) ......... Buy cards, relics, potions, remove cards
  Event (? icon) ........... Random choice encounter with tradeoffs

8.3 Act Progression

  After defeating an Act boss, the player advances to the next act with a fresh
  map. After Act 3's boss (THE DEVIL), the game is won.

8.4 Card Reward Rarity Weights

                Common    Uncommon    Rare
  Act 1         70%       25%         5%
  Act 2         50%       35%         15%
  Act 3         35%       35%         30%
  Elite         0%        70-95%      5-30%
  Boss          0%        0%          100%

  Reward offers 3 cards (4 with Scout's Emblem). Player picks 1 (2 with
  Collector's Tome), or skips.


9. CARD UPGRADE SYSTEM
------------------------

Available at Rest Sites. Three upgrade paths:

9.1 Sharpen
  Creature: +2 ATK (+3 with Blacksmith's Hammer)
  Spell:    +2 spell damage value (+3 with Blacksmith's Hammer)

9.2 Fortify
  Creature: +2 HP (+3 with Blacksmith's Hammer)
  Spell:    -1 mana cost (min 0)

9.3 Imbue
  Creature: Add keyword from [Piercing, Swift, Thorns, Regenerate].
            2 random choices shown (3 with Blacksmith's Hammer).
  Spell:    Choose: Add Retain keyword, OR double effect value + add Exhaust

Upgraded cards display with "+" suffix (e.g., "Footman +"). Upgrades tracked
per deck index via UID system and applied at draw time.


10. SHOP
---------

Prices (base):
  Common card ......... 50g
  Uncommon card ....... 75g
  Rare card ........... 120g
  Relic ............... 100g
  Healing Potion ...... 40g
  Card Removal ........ 50g

Merchant's License relic applies 25% discount. Scavenger's Pouch grants +20g
on card removal.

Stock: 4 cards (act-based rarity weights) + 1 relic + potion + removal.

Shop Rarity Weights:
                Common    Uncommon    Rare
  Act 1         70%       25%         5%
  Act 2         50%       35%         15%
  Act 3         35%       35%         30%


11. REST SITES
---------------

Three options (pick one):
  1. REST ...... Heal to full HP
  2. UPGRADE ... Choose a card, then choose Sharpen / Fortify / Imbue
  3. REMOVE .... Remove one card from deck


12. EVENTS (8)
---------------

  The Blacksmith's Offer
    A: Upgrade any card. That card gains Exhaust (one use per fight)
    B: Leave

  The Blood Fountain
    A: Full heal. Lose 3 max HP permanently
    B: Gain 5 max HP. Take 5 damage

  The Collector
    A: Gain random rare card + gain a Curse
    B: Leave

  Dark Altar
    A: Remove up to 3 cards. Lose 3 max HP per card removed
    B: Leave

  The Gambler
    A: Reveal top 5 cards of your deck. Discard any number permanently
       (removed from the run)
    B: Leave

  The Hermit
    A: Remove any card. Next 2 card rewards offer 1 fewer choice
    B: Transform a random card into a random card of one higher rarity

  The Butcher
    A: Choose creature: +2 ATK, gains Wither 1
    B: Leave

  Mysterious Shrine
    A: Choose a card to remove. Gain a random relic and a Curse
    B: Leave

Events are non-repeating within a run (tracks seen). If all 8 seen, pool resets.
